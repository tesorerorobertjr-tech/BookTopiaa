The Dragon Next Door

Milo expects a quiet summer until a tiny dragon lands in his laundry basket and asks for help hiding from royal hunters.

The Dragon Next Door is a story about fantasy, small choices, and the unexpected ways people change. At first, the answer seems simple: take one step and hope for the best. But the journey asks Pao to look closer, listen carefully, and trust the quiet feeling that something meaningful is possible.

By the end of that day, nothing is exactly the same. The mystery is not that life suddenly becomes easy; it is that courage often looks ordinary from the outside. It is choosing to speak, to stay, to leave, or to begin again—even when the next page is uncertain.

And so the moment passes, leaving behind a lesson that is easy to forget and worth remembering: the smallest decision can open a door to a whole new world.
